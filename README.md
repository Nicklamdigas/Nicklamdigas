### 🛠️ What I'm Building & Working On

* ☕ **Coffee & Macadamia Estate OS** *(Flagship Project — FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite/PostgreSQL, Offline-First PWA)*  
  An end-to-end farm management and traceability system architected as a **Modular Monolith** for a 150-acre estate:
  * **Land & Agronomy Store:** Block-level EUDR GPS polygons, batch-tracked fertilizer/chemical inventory, and automated Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) chemical quarantine locks.
  * **Workforce & Kenyan Payroll Engine:** Offline-capable field weighing sync (`local_uuid` idempotency + `409` optimistic concurrency control), human-in-the-loop supervisor approval gates, and statutory deductions (SHIF, NSSF Tier I/II, Housing Levy, PAYE).
  * **Wet & Dry Mill Traceability:** Full lot lineage from harvested cherry (`HARVESTED` → `PULPED` P1/P2/P3/Mbuni → `DRYING` `<= 10.5%` moisture gate → County Movement Permits → Commercial Dry Mill outturns `AA/AB/PB` & cupping scores → NCE/Direct Settlement System ledger).
  * **Explainable Analytics (Kenya AI Bill 2026 Aligned):** Local Ridge Regression yield forecasting, fertilizer budget optimization, and picker/processing loss anomaly detection with human-in-the-loop review.
